Request account deletion

To request permanent deletion, email snippo.support@gmail.com from the address associated with your Snippo account.

Support will verify that you own the account and ask you to explicitly confirm deletion. Do not send passwords, verification links, codes, tokens, or snippet contents.

Deletion permanently removes your account and the snippets it owns. To end your current session and keep your account and snippets, use Sign out from your library instead.
